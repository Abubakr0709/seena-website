Privacy Policy

How we handle your personal data and cookies.

This policy explains what personal data Seena Integrative Health Centre collects through this website, by phone, WhatsApp, email and our event forms, why we use it, and what you can ask us to do with it. Last updated: 18 November 2026.

Rua das Musas 13A, Parque das Nações, Lisbon, Portugal. For any privacy question, write to info@seenahealth.com or call +351 924 353 219.

What We Collect

Data you give us

Bookings and questions. When you call, message us on WhatsApp or email us, we receive your name, phone number or email address, and whatever you write to us. We use it to arrange and manage your appointments. The legal basis is taking steps to provide the service you asked for (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)).

Event registrations. When you reserve a seat at Seena Table or another event through our Google Form, we receive the answers you enter there. We use them to organise the event and contact you about it.

Health information. Your practitioner records the health information you share during consultations and treatments. We process it to provide your care, under GDPR Art. 9(2)(h), and keep it confidential under the professional secrecy rules that apply to health providers in Portugal. Please don't send detailed health information through the website, Instagram or WhatsApp; your practitioner will ask for what they need in person.

Cookies

Analytics, only with your consent

When you first visit, a banner asks whether you accept analytics cookies. If you click Reject, the site loads no tracking tools. If you click Accept, Google Tag Manager loads two tools: Google Analytics, which shows us which pages people visit and how they reach us, and the Meta Pixel, which measures visits from our Facebook and Instagram ads. We ask again after six months, and you can change your choice any time with the Cookie settings link at the bottom of each page.

Who Else Sees It

Service providers

We share data only with the companies whose tools we use: Google (Analytics, Tag Manager, Forms), Meta (WhatsApp, Meta Pixel) and GitHub, which hosts this website and records basic technical data such as IP addresses to deliver and protect it. Some of these companies process data in the United States under the EU-US Data Privacy Framework. We don't sell your data.

How Long We Keep It

Retention

We keep booking messages and event registrations while we need them to manage your appointments or the event, and delete them afterwards. Clinical records stay on file for the period Portuguese health regulations require. Google Analytics keeps visit data for [2 or 14 months, to confirm in the Analytics account].

Your Rights

What you can ask us

You can ask us for a copy of your data, to correct it, delete it, limit how we use it, or send it to you in a portable format. You can also object to how we use it, and withdraw consent at any time without affecting what we did before. Write to info@seenahealth.com and we will reply within one month.

You can also complain to the Portuguese data protection authority, the Comissão Nacional de Proteção de Dados, at www.cnpd.pt.
